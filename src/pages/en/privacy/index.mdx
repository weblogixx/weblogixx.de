## In short

This website sets no cookies, uses no tracking or analytics services and loads no content from
third-party servers. The fonts are served by this website itself, too.

## Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by {hoster.name}, {hoster.address}. When you visit it, the host processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time and the requested page, to deliver the website and protect it against abuse (server logs). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is a secure and reliable website.

The host processes this data on my behalf (Art. 28 GDPR). Details: <a href={hoster.privacyUrl}>{hoster.name}'s privacy policy</a>.

## Settings in your browser

Two settings are stored only when you change them yourself, and only in your browser's local storage
(localStorage): your light/dark mode choice (`sunny-mode`) and the skills view on the home
page (`stack-all`). They are never sent to me or anyone else. Storing them is necessary for
the feature you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete them at any time in your browser
settings.

## Contact by email

If you email me, I process your details to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for inquiries about
a possible project, otherwise (f)). I delete them once they're no longer needed for that and no legal
retention periods apply.

## Links to other websites

Links to other services (e.g. GitHub or LinkedIn) are plain links. Only when you follow one does that
provider's privacy policy apply.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing of your data, to
data portability and to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Just
write to the email address above. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory
authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
