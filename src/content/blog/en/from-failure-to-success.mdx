The <Link href="https://blackforest-ux.de">Black Forest UX e.V.</Link> is a club that connects <Abbr title="User Interfaces/User Experience">UI/UX</Abbr> enthusiasts and professionals across southern Baden-Württemberg.
I was thrilled when they invited <Link href={route('career', 'en', 'sick')}>SICK's</Link> <Link href="https://github.com/synergy-design-system/synergy-design-system/graphs/contributors?from=2023-01-01T23%3A00%3A00.000Z">Synergy Design System team</Link> to a <Link href="https://blackforest-ux.de/veranstaltung/vom-scheitern-zum-erfolg-die-evolution-des-design-systems-bei-sick/">talk about the evolution of design systems</Link>.

## A little bit of stage fright

I got a little bit of stage fright before the talk.
After all, the club consists of true specialists, masters of their craft.
Imagine around 30 professionals grilling you in front of your teammates (and your boss, too).

My colleague Sebastian guided us nicely through a talk full of humor and hard-won wisdom that lasted a full hour.
People laughed and engaged in discussions.
My nervosity dropped.
A bit too early as it turned out.

## Do not try this at home

After the original talk, it was planned that I should showcase our use cases for AI in design systems for about five minutes.
A small outro basically.
In the end, it became a talk on its own, thirty minutes.
I highlighted the importance of a good data model and core rules and how these can be applied as guard rails when using AI.

- Curating a set of rules for each of your components,
- Seeing your own design system through the users eyes, may he be a designer or frontend developer,
- providing guidance and patterns into AI systems so they have usage samples,
- Making all of this machine readable,

Finally, I was given the possibility to showcase all this in a custom tailored demo project.
We build forms with and without agentic knowledge and compared the solutions.
I prepared this small demo a couple of days before and it worked out fine.
Confident, I instructed my agent to fix a broken form to showcase our design system's ability to lint frontend patterns.

Usually, I prepare a video for things like this, just because you can skip forward if something takes too long or breaks.
One of the reasons I was nervous at all was that we wanted to show a live demo of the system instead.

It came like it had to: It took ages.
Turns out my prompt was written with a scope far too wide: Instead of just assessing the form, it became a test for the whole demo application.
While the agent wrote playwright based integration tests nobody had asked for, laughter arose.
I was forced to cancel the whole process and rewrite my prompt for better results.
The second version eventually would ran through just fine, but still brought me to a conclusion:

> Let me be clear: I will **not** do live AI demos in front of an audience ever again!
> <cite>Me, after multiple live AI demos failed during the talk</cite>

## Design Systems and AI - a match made in heaven?

AI is getting more and more traction in development, and the frontend world is no exception to this.
The difference between generated backend and frontend code is quite easy to grasp.
While suboptimal code in an API may be known to the developer, each vibe-coded frontend project is directly visible to the customer and may look like an outlier to a customer also using other applications created by your company.

AI may know your button's interface, but it might just guess which variant it should use for a given use case.

The model has seen thousands of forms, but it does not know about your wording rules or error placement.
It might be able to format tabular data just right, but it does not know how to apply your carefully crafted master/detail view pattern.

## What I took away from it

Fortunately, this is where design systems shine.
Big companies specialized in design systems like <Link href="https://zeroheight.com/resource/the-state-of-ai-in-design-systems/">zeroheight</Link> are already mapping out ways to bring design systems closer to agents.
At <Link href={route('career', 'en', 'sick')}>SICK</Link>, Synergy will continue doing so, too.
The design system of the future must be consumable by agentic software, be it through <Abbr title="Model Context Protocol">MCP</Abbr> servers or local skills.
